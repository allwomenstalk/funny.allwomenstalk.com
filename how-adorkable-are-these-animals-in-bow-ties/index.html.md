# 30 Adorkable Animals in Bow Ties That Prove Pets Wear Ties Best

The 11th Doctor isn't the only one who looks smashing in neckwear! Whether it's a dapper puppy or a sleek feline, there is something utterly adorkable about **animals in bow ties**. From red-carpet duds to everyday flair, these stylish cuties know how to keep it classy. If you've been looking for fashion inspiration or just a reason to smile, seeing **animals wearing ties** is guaranteed to brighten your day according to our popular [funny pet photo galleries](https://funny.allwomenstalk.com/).

Before styling your own furry companion, keep these quick safety tips in mind according to official [ASPCA pet safety guidelines](https://www.aspca.org):

- Always ensure neckwear is loose enough for two fingers to fit comfortably.

- Choose lightweight clip-on or breakaway velcro ties over tight elastic.

- Never leave dressed-up pets unsupervised during play or outdoor activities.
