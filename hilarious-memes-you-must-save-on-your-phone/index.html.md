# 7 Memes to Save on Your Phone for Every Group Chat

Sometimes responding with words is not enough; words just don’t fully express everything that you want to say, which is why hilarious memes often come in handy. They are straight to the point, funny, and effective for carrying out everything you have to say during a quick phone conversation. Plus, saving memes to your device is just fun when chatting between friends! Having a stash of phone memes funny enough for any occasion spices up every conversation and brings more laughter to your day.

Here is why keeping a dedicated library for saving memes on your phone is a total game-changer for digital communication:

- Delivers perfect comedic timing in fast-moving text threads
- Replaces long, awkward explanations with instant visual humor
- Enhances nonverbal communication memes and emotional context

## 1. Wat

![Wat](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/zl/ep/kb1gg0uo_500x498.jpg)

We have all received texts and messages from friends while talking on the phone or texting that make no sense at all. It is hard to decipher what they are trying to say and it is even harder to decide how to reply. However, with a few hilarious memes at your disposal such as this one, you will be able to reply quickly with the addition of a comedic twist. According to [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/wat), it is “the only proper response to something that makes absolutely no sense.”

## 2. Watch out

![Watch out](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/l1/ss/v5zxp8b3_600x436.jpg)

This hilarious rage comic character with a defensive gesture is usually used to mock boastful statements made by Internet users. When someone sends an exaggerated brag in a text thread, pairing it with a [baffled reaction image](#1) calls out the absurdity instantly. It originally stemmed from a still image of astrophysicist Neil deGrasse Tyson during an interview and was later associated with the iconic catchphrase, “Watch out, we got a badass over here!”

## 3. Rage Face

![Rage Face](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/zg/zg/yay3j62a_600x338.jpg)

The Rage Face meme is often used to express a variety of intense emotions such as astonishment, rage, and sudden revelation. This image first appeared in a classic rage comic, but the original artist behind it remains unknown. Nonetheless, it has been shared all over the Internet and became one of the most recognizable phone memes funny internet culture has ever produced.

| Meme Name | Best Situation to Use | Key Emotional Vibe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wat | Baffling text messages | Pure confusion |
| Watch out | Mocking online brags | Sarcastic caution |
| Rage Face | Unexpected chaos | Shocked realization |

## 4. Mother of God

![Mother of God](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/pl/tz/id7sx0r7_548x394.jpg)

This is one of my personal favorites when it comes to memes to save on your phone. I can’t even count how many times I have used it in past text conversations. It is one of the best nonverbal communication memes to express your shock or disbelief in a very original way. Plus, it is just completely hilarious whenever dramatic news drops in the group chat!

## 5. Not Sure if

![Not Sure if](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/kb/r8/b8okd1dv_304x228.jpg)

The following meme, taken from a still of Fry in Futurama, is often used to represent internal dialogue and healthy skepticism. It is frequently accompanied by text following the classic template: “Not sure if... or just...”. When receiving uncertain information during a phone conversation meme debate, having this image saved to phone meme folders brings immediate clarity and humor. If you are doubting a claim, much like the [Mother of God reaction](#4), Fry's signature squint says it all. You can read more about its history on [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/futurama-fry).

## 6. I Don’t Know

![I Don’t Know](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/w1/fh/u36f3fcz_600x440.jpg)

Before the dog version of this meme first came out, people expressed their sense of confusion by using a simple stick figure with shrugging arms. However, once a picture of this clueless puppy was uploaded from a Korean image board site, it blew up across social media. The Internet simply can’t resist cute puppies, making this one of the essential memes to have on your phone when you genuinely have no answer.

## 7. Oh, Stop It!

![Oh, Stop It!](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/gy/qe/0dbia2jw_300x243.jpg)

The “Oh Stop It You” character first appeared on the GODBEAMS blog and was later featured prominently across webcomics. Today, it is used to convey playful self-modesty after receiving compliments or praise while communicating over text. Unlike the loud astonishment of a [Rage Face shock](#3), this image adds a flattering, warm tone to any exchange. Save it on your device and you will definitely get plenty of use out of it.

With such a widespread use of mobile technology and nonverbal communication memes, saving memes directly to your camera roll makes staying connected effortless. Plus, sharing memes while talking on the phone memes or texting continues to be a staple among friends. What are some of your favorite memes to keep saved on your phone?
