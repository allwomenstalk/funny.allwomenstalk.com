# 7 Things You Should Never Say to a Man \(And How to Communicate Better\)

There are certain comments you should avoid saying to a man unless you are looking to start an argument. If you want to keep the peace and build healthy communication, keeping a few filter-less thoughts to yourself goes a long way. After all, nobody enjoys hearing comments that spark unnecessary friction. To help keep your relationship smooth and supportive, here are seven phrases you should avoid saying to your partner.

## 1. You're like a Brother to Me

If a guy has feelings for you, this statement will instantly crush his hopes. While you might intend it as a compliment about how close and safe you feel around him, all he hears is that he has been permanently placed in the friend zone and that romantic intimacy is off the table. Even if you are trying to be affectionate, comparing a man to a family member is a comment that rarely lands well if there is any romantic interest involved.

## 2. We Need to Talk

Unless you are planning to end the relationship on the spot, avoid using this dreaded opening line. The moment those four words leave your mouth, his mind will automatically jump to worst-case scenarios. If you need to discuss something important, reframe your intro by stating the topic directly instead of triggering instant anxiety. When [discussing sensitive relationship topics](#3), clear and specific phrasing keeps tension low.

## 3. My Ex Loved That

If you are watching a movie and casually mention that your ex was obsessed with Jennifer Aniston, your partner will likely remember that comment for a long time. Every single time an episode of [Friends](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0108778/) airs or a trailer for [Horrible Bosses 2](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2170439/) pops up, he might be subtly reminded of your past dating history. It is usually best to keep nostalgic details about previous partners to yourself to prevent unnecessary comparisons.

| Phrase to Avoid | Potential Impact | Better Approach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "You're like a brother" | Kills romantic spark | Express friendship without family labels |
| "We need to talk" | Causes instant panic | State the topic clearly and calmly |
| "My ex loved that" | Spawns comparisons | Keep past relationships out of it |

## 4. You Throw like a Girl

Using outdated gendered cliches as insults is unnecessary and counterproductive. Women excel at sports and athletic endeavors every single day, so using female gender terms as a punchline makes little sense. Rather than bruising his ego, relying on lazy stereotypes just makes the speaker look out of touch.

## 5. You're Mine

While some people view possessive statements as affectionate, others interpret them as overly controlling or objectifying. Partners are equal companions, not personal property. Expressing devotion is wonderful, but framing it around healthy partnership rather than ownership creates a much healthier dynamic. Just as with [handling delicate conversations](#2), using balanced language respects individual autonomy.

Key principles for healthy relationship communication:

- Focus on how you feel rather than labeling your partner.
- Avoid bringing up past relationships during casual moments.
- Give reassurance before addressing serious topics.

## 6. I'll Return It

When someone takes the time to pick out a gift for you, showing gratitude is basic courtesy. Immediately tossing a present aside to ask for a return receipt can feel unappreciative and deflate his effort. Appreciate the gesture first, and if needed, gently guide gift ideas before the next special occasion arrives.

## 7. Your Friend is Hot

Few people want to hear their partner openly gush about how attractive their close friends are. While it is completely normal to admire celebrities like [Leonardo DiCaprio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonardo_DiCaprio), openly complimenting someone in his immediate circle can spark unnecessary insecurity. Keeping those observations private helps maintain trust, much like [avoiding constant comparisons to past partners](#3).

Maintaining open, respectful communication is key to a lasting relationship. Sometimes, taking a brief pause before speaking saves both of you from avoidable arguments. What innocent comments have you said that turned out to be surprisingly sensitive? Share your thoughts and experiences with us!
